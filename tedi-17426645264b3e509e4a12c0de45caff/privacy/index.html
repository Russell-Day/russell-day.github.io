Privacy Policy for personal-project (TEDI)

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how personal-project (also known as TEDI), developed by Russell Day ("we", "our", or "the application"), handles, stores, and protects your information when you connect Google services.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you explicitly grant permission via Google OAuth, personal-project accesses the following data:

  • Google Calendar Data: We access your calendar metadata, event titles, descriptions, locations, and start/end times through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope to display your schedule and allow you to create new events.
  • Gmail Data: We access message identifiers, sender and recipient headers, timestamps, and message snippets/bodies through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scopes to organize and summarize communications and send email drafts you approve.
  • Basic Profile Information: We access your primary email address to identify which account corresponds to which calendar and inbox feed.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your Google user data is used exclusively to deliver prominent, user-facing productivity features inside the personal-project application, specifically:

  • Rendering your daily schedule and agenda on your personal dashboard.
  • Allowing you to schedule and organize upcoming events.
  • Categorizing and triaging incoming messages into personal, academic, and notification categories.
  • Enabling local drafting and user-approved sending of emails.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

personal-project’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Storage & Local-First Processing

personal-project is architected as a local-first software application:

  • Local Storage Only: All OAuth tokens, cached calendar events, and email snippets are stored exclusively on your local computer filesystem.
  • No External Servers: We do not operate external application servers, central databases, or cloud analytics backends for personal-project.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: Your Google user data is never transferred, transmitted, or proxied through third-party servers. All API communications occur directly between your machine and Google's official endpoints over encrypted HTTPS.

4. Prohibitions on Data Sale, Advertising, and AI Model Training

  • No Selling or Leasing: We will NEVER sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data to any data brokers, advertisers, or third parties.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used, transferred, or disclosed for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is never used or transferred to train generalized, non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

5. Human Access to Data

Human operators do not read or view your Google user data. Data is processed solely through local automated scripts on your device, except when you explicitly view your own data on your screen or if required by applicable legal process.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain full control over the retention of your data:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can wipe all cached data, configuration files, and authentication tokens at any time simply by deleting the local application files on your device.
  • Revoking Access: You can immediately revoke personal-project's access to your Google Account at any time through Google Account Security Settings.

7. Security Practices

Authentication tokens and cached data stored on your local workstation are protected by your operating system's standard file permission controls. All communications with Google APIs use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
