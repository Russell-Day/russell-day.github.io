TEDI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

TEDI is a private, local-first personal assistant developed by Russell Day for personal productivity management.

1. Data We Access

When you connect your Google Account, TEDI requests access to:

  • Google Calendar API: To view, create, and organize calendar events on your primary and secondary calendars.
  • Gmail API: To retrieve email snippets and drafts for local triage and categorization.

2. How Data Is Stored & Used

  • Local Processing Only: All Google user data is processed and stored exclusively on the user's local, private hardware.
  • No External Transmission: Data is never uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party tracking services.
  • No Data Selling or Advertising: User data is never sold, leased, or used for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Users have complete control over their data at all times:

  • Local cache files and tokens can be deleted immediately by the user on their device at any time.
  • Google Account access can be revoked at any time via Google Account Permissions.

4. Contact Information
