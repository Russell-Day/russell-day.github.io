Application Overview

personal-project (TEDI)

A local-first personal productivity and life assistant for unified scheduling, email triage, and task management.

Purpose of the Application

personal-project (also known as TEDI) is a private, local productivity application designed to simplify personal schedule planning, academic deadlines, and daily communications into a single, cohesive dashboard.

Instead of manually checking multiple calendar feeds, university portals, and personal email accounts separately, personal-project unifies these workflows on the user's private computer without transmitting any personal data to external cloud services or third parties.

Key Features & Functionality

📅 Calendar Management

Consolidates personal, work, and university Google Calendars into daily, 3-day, week, and monthly views. Allows quick event creation, agenda summaries, and schedule conflict resolution.

📬 Email Triage

Retrieves incoming email headers and snippets via the Gmail API to categorize notifications, highlight urgent messages, and draft replies with user confirmation.

✅ Task & Academic Planning

Combines coursework deadlines, assignments, and personal todos alongside calendar events to create daily focus agendas.

🔒 Local-First Architecture

All data retrieved from connected APIs is processed and cached strictly on the user's local workstation. No external telemetry or cloud databases are used.

How Google User Data Is Used

When you authenticate your Google Account with personal-project, the application requests access to specific Google APIs strictly to provide the core productivity features listed above:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar): Used to read calendar events so your daily agenda is displayed on the dashboard, and to create new events directly from your schedule view.
  • Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send): Used to read incoming message snippets for inbox categorization, label urgent messages, and send user-approved email drafts.

Notice: personal-project does NOT require you to log in to browse this information. All information on this page and our privacy terms are publicly accessible.
