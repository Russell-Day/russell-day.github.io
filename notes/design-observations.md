# Interaction study — Steven Yu’s site

Observed with Playwright on https://stevenyuyy.com/ on 2026-09-19.
This is an interaction/design study, not a request to reproduce his implementation.

## What I actually tried

- Entered `attention` in the contact sentence. The fish assembled around the word, with a short confirmation below it.
- Clicked `DNA` and `protein` within the About paragraph. The fish formed a molecular visualization in the portrait area. The surrounding page stayed recognizable.
- Opened MiniSteve through the contact input and asked “What does Steven like building?” Saw the thinking state, the school forming answer text, and the answer in the expandable chat history.
- Clicked the name seal and an empty area of the hero to explore the fish response.
- Inspected the decorative sequence and its public script. It highlights TATAAA within the divider; the footer calls it the page’s promoter. This is a visual biology reference, not functioning biological sequence annotation. No claim that its position is biologically exact.

## Why it works (interpretation)

1. **A recurring material.** The fish serve as atmosphere, feedback, illustration, and assistant output. Visitors build familiarity with one system instead of learning unrelated widgets.
2. **Responses stay connected to their triggers.** The ring forms around the word you typed. Molecular shapes appear beside the paragraph you are reading.
3. **A useful page underneath.** Small navigation, restrained prose, readable project entries. The playfulness has space because the rest of the layout is quiet.
4. **Specificity.** Name pronunciation, research vocabulary, and understated captions connect details to the owner. They are not generic portfolio decorations.
5. **An invitation followed by a reward.** Underlined words and short hints invite a little investigation. Discoveries can unfold over time.
6. **Movement has continuity.** Fish travel into arrangements; they are not replaced by a new illustration. This was missing from our dog prototype.

## Direction for Russell

Use the existing multimodal network as the recurring visual material. Keep daylight’s blue/ivory palette with gold signals. Let concrete details about the work provide the small discoveries; avoid marketing slogans and overly personal invented prose.

Implemented first step: restore the original network geometry and moving signals, with hover/focus/tap disclosure and a short explanation tied to the research. Keep the site usable on touch and with reduced motion. Move evening/daylight control into the header. Correct the Amazon internship to May–July 2026 and remove the disliked medicine paragraph.

- A tiny, optional “what is this measuring?” disclosure on research metrics, using the actual study limitations and denominators when available.
